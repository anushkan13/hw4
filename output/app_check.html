Check 2

Dynamic product cards populate after a category question

Asked in the chat, on the Products page: “what hoodies do you have?”

What this proves: the agent returns matches as structured data (ChatReply.products), not prose, and the panel renders whatever that list contains. Because the cards are built from catalogue rows, a product the model invented could not appear here — and the names, prices, and images shown are the shop’s real ones.
Check 3 · Usability upgrade from Problem 9

Sold-out size suggestions: an honest “no”, plus a way forward

Asked in the chat, on the Benjamin Franklin 1/4 Zip product page — whose XL is genuinely out of stock: “Do you have this in XL?”

What this proves: the assistant never softens a sold-out answer into “limited availability”, and it never ends on a dead end. After a sold-out result it calls find_alternatives_in_size, which returns only same-category products that actually have that size in stock, sorted by closeness in price. This matters commercially: 145 of the 612 size rows in inventory are at zero, so roughly a quarter of size questions would otherwise end in a lost sale.
